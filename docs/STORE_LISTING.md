## Full description

Stop typing the same thing over and over.

QuickReply saves the messages you write every day and inserts them in any text field when you type a short shortcut. Type /hello and get a full greeting. Type /pricing and get your pricing reply. It works in Gmail, web forms and the text editors most websites use.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Save snippets with a name, a shortcut and any amount of text, including multiple lines
• Type the shortcut anywhere and it expands instantly. Ctrl+Z brings the shortcut back
• Add today's date or time automatically with {date}, {time} or {datetime}
• Add fill-in fields like {name} or {company} and complete them in a small form as you insert
• Organize snippets in folders, star favorites, see recently used snippets, search and sort
• Turn individual snippets on or off, pause QuickReply everywhere, or turn it off on specific websites
• Export a backup file, import it on another computer, or restore it later
• Light, dark and system themes

BUILT FOR PRIVACY
• Your snippets are stored only in your browser
• No account, no analytics; your snippets stay in your browser
• Nothing you type is recorded or sent anywhere
• Password, payment and one-time-code fields are always ignored

GOOD TO KNOW
• Some apps draw text in their own way instead of using standard text fields (for example Google Docs). Expansion may not work there.
• Similar shortcuts like /hi and /hire never collide: the shorter one waits for a space.
• If a site already uses / for its own commands, choose ; as your prefix in Settings.

QuickReply is free for up to 5 snippets; Pro removes the limit. Chrome is a trademark of Google LLC.
